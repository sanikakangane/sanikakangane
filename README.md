<br/><br/>

# I don't just want to know *what*.

# I want to understand *why*.

</div>

I'm Sanika, a B.Tech Computer Science student with a mind that rarely stops asking questions.

I'm fascinated by the invisible logic behind the things we use every day — how applications think, how data moves, how systems communicate, and how artificial intelligence is changing the way we approach problems.

I explore technology by experimenting with it, breaking ideas into smaller pieces, and building my understanding one concept at a time.

I'm still learning. That's the interesting part.
